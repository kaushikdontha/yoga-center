## 3. Managing Content

### 3.1. Uploading Photos and Videos

To upload new photos or videos (e.g., for the instructor profile, gallery, or other sections):

1.  Log in to the Admin Panel (as described above).
2.  Look for a section related to "Media," "Gallery," "Photos," or "Uploads" in the navigation menu.
3.  Follow the on-screen instructions to upload your media files. You may be able to:
    *   Drag and drop files.
    *   Click an "Upload" button to select files from your computer.
4.  After uploading, ensure you save or publish the changes for them to appear on the live website.

### 3.2. Updating Text Content (e.g., Pricing, Batch Details)

If your website has dynamic content sections that can be updated through the admin panel (e.g., membership pricing, class batch times, instructor details, testimonials):

1.  Log in to the Admin Panel.
2.  Navigate to the relevant section (e.g., "Pricing," "Batches," "Instructors," "Testimonials").
3.  You should find forms or editable fields where you can modify the text content.
4.  After making changes, remember to click "Save" or "Update" to apply them.
